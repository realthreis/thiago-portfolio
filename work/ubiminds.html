Growth Director, North America · Aug 2023 – Apr 2025

Building the US Demand Engine for a LatAm Staff Augmentation Brand

Events, an owned event property, and a content engine built around what a CTO actually needs to see before trusting a staffing partner.

Context

Ubiminds helps US companies build nearshore engineering teams in Latin America without setting up a foreign legal entity — "Talent-as-a-Service" for CTOs and VPs of Engineering who need to scale a technical team fast, with cultural and timezone alignment. I led go-to-market strategy, demand generation, and sales enablement for the US market, building a cross-functional team spanning marketing, SDR, events, and growth across US and LatAm time zones.

The challenge

Nearshore staff augmentation is a trust-dependent category before it's anything else — a CTO handing hiring authority to an outside partner needs proof of technical fit and cultural alignment, not just a lower rate card. Ubiminds was competing for mindshare against larger, better-known staffing players in a US market where the brand had to earn credibility from a standing start.

What I built

A sustained events presence, not a one-off booth A calendar-year presence at the events where the actual buyers show up: EdTech Week NY, FETC, TCEA, SXSW 2024, eMerge Americas, Bett Educar, and RD Summit — repeated, cumulative visibility instead of a one-time spend.
A proprietary event property, not just rented booth space In 2024, I was part of the founding team that launched Connecting the Americas — an event series Ubiminds owns outright, rather than another line item on someone else's sponsorship package.
An inbound content engine built around the buyer's actual decision points Cost-per-hire economics, MSA/SOW structuring, nearshore vs. offshore tradeoffs, how to manage a remote team across cultures. Nine blog articles, an 11-page "Supercharge Your Software Team" buyer's guide ebook, a 26-page sales-ready staff augmentation proposal deck, and supporting thought-leadership pieces.
Sales collateral and ABM sequences built for one buyer persona Everything — the proposal deck, the ebook, the outbound sequences — was built around what a CTO or VP of Engineering specifically needs to see before they'll trust a staffing partner with their hiring.
