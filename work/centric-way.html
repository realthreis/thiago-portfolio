Executive Director Brazil + Sales & Marketing US · Apr 2018 – Apr 2023

Scaling Brazil's American High School Category — From 14 Schools to a National Media Story

A category that didn't have a name yet in Brazil, built through earned credibility and academic results that did the marketing on their own.

Context

Dual mandate: full P&L ownership of the Brazilian operation of WAY American School (Centric Learning's international brand) plus commercial leadership for the US market expansion (Texas, Florida). WAY delivers a US high school diploma inside partner private schools in Brazil — hybrid in-person/online, built on Centric's HERO project-based learning platform, accredited by Cognia. I managed all commercial, marketing, legal, and financial operations with direct accountability to the US founders.

The challenge

"An American high school diploma without leaving Brazil" wasn't a category buyers were searching for by name in 2018 — it had to be built, not just marketed. That meant proving institutional credibility (accreditation, real academic outcomes) and creating market awareness simultaneously, while running the actual school operation day to day: 14 partner schools, hundreds of students, real families making a real decision about their kids' education.

What I built

Scaled the partner network from 14 toward 40 schools in Year 1 The 2018–2019 strategic plan I built targeted 31 new schools and 12 new cities on top of the existing 14-partner, 406-student footprint — landing the network close to that 40-school mark within the first year. The enrolled student base grew even faster than the school count: from 400 to 950 actively enrolled students in just 8 months in 2019. Over the full 2018–2023 tenure, WAY served over 2,000 students in Brazil, sustaining 35–38 partner schools and 123 corporate-wide employees (80 certified teachers).
Became the recurring national media voice for a category that didn't exist yet Repeated, unpaid editorial coverage across Brazil's largest outlets — four separate features on Terra.com.br (one of Brazil's top-3 web portals), plus Correio Braziliense, Metrópoles, and a 10+ page feature in The Knowledge Review naming WAY the "Best American High School in Brazil 2022." Also a recurring guest on the Papo de Educador education podcast, speaking on the microschool/podschool trend and COVID-era distance learning.
Led the school through COVID without losing the year Directed the shift to fully online delivery while keeping WAY's US-based remote model (running since 2009) as the backbone. The pivot included a structured second-semester return plan built on cited local risk data (a Datafolha survey showing 76% of parents feared sending kids back), not guesswork.
Ran the Texas market entry with the same research-first discipline As Taskforce Lead Strategist on a dedicated Texas team, I built a market-entry plan around Texas-specific product lines (HB4545 tutoring, credit recovery, SPED/ELL, career-technical education) and ran a real sales pipeline across dozens of Texas ISDs — from zero to one qualified demo per week by the second half of 2022.
Built a portfolio-prioritization system for partner schools A BCG-matrix-style scoring framework — Partner Potential × Partner Size × WAY Performance (conversion share) — tracked across every partner school and city. The same "built the management system, not just the campaign" instinct behind the Laureate maturity scorecard years earlier.
Delivered academic results that did the marketing for us WAY students consistently scored above the US national SAT average — 1268 in 2021, 1274 in 2022, against a US average around 1059–1060 both years, landing in the top 1% multiple years running. In 2022 alone, WAY students won R$3 million in US university scholarships, and across 2021–2022 the school posted 100% acceptance across 39 international university applications, including a full scholarship offer from Duke University and a full-ride Lester B. Pearson Scholarship from the University of Toronto.
Maintained my own standing as a volunteer Cognia-credentialed evaluator Concurrent with running WAY, I volunteered as a named evaluator on real Cognia accreditation site-visit teams — a credential about my own standing in the field of international education accreditation, not a reflection of WAY's own accreditation score (which is a separate, and separately strong, result).
